# Name and logo

The code of Wintor is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later: you may study, change, fork and redistribute it.
The **name "Wintor" and the Wintor logo are not covered by that license** (GPL-3.0, section 7(e)). They tell
users that a build comes from this project, so they are reserved.

## You may

- Say that your project is "based on Wintor" or "a fork of Wintor".
- Use the name to refer to this project, e.g. in articles, reviews and tutorials.
- Build and run the unmodified source for yourself, logo included.

## You may not, without permission

- Publish a modified version under the name Wintor, or a confusingly similar name, or with the Wintor logo.
  A fork must have its own name and icon (change `Package.appxmanifest`, the logo in
  `tools/Wintor.IconTool` and the strings `AppDisplayName` in every `Resources.resw`).
- Publish any build — modified or not — in an app store (Microsoft Store, winget, others) under the Wintor name.
  Official builds come only from the sources listed in the README.

Apps that pretend to be Wintor are reported to the store for removal.

Questions or requests: open an issue.
