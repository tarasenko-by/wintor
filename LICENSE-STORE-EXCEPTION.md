## Why

Some app store terms are considered incompatible with the plain GPL. Without this permission, every contributor
would have to agree separately before a build containing their code could be published in the Microsoft Store.
The permission does not make anything less free: the source stays public, and forks are still GPL.

It covers the license only. The **name and logo** are a separate matter — see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md):
a fork may be published in a store under its own name.
